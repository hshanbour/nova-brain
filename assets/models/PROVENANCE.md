# Nova Aether prototype asset provenance

- `nova-female-base.glb`: generated MakeHuman base body and facial assets from `kblood/CharacterCreator`, repository `output/base_body.glb`, retrieved 2026-11-06.
- `nova-hair-bob.glb`: MakeHuman `bob02` hair from the same repository, retrieved 2026-11-06.
- Upstream asset declarations are preserved in `asset_licenses.json`. The body, skin, eyes, eyebrows, eyelashes and `bob02` hair are declared CC0 by the upstream build manifest.
- Upstream source: https://github.com/kblood/CharacterCreator (generated output assets are documented as CC0-1.0).

The models provide authored anatomy only. Nova's materials, topology overlay, neural halo, data strands, state animation and audio responsiveness are implemented locally in `assets/premium-avatar.js`.
